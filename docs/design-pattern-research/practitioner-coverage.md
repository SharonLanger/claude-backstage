## Static route walkthroughs

These are inspections of local routes and actual entry content under the stated constraints, not live Claude conversations.

### Deleted regression test and a new bug (P-S01a, P-S01b)

- **Constraints and distinction.** CLI user can inspect the diff and run a trusted baseline; can restrict writes if necessary. Was the test change justified by a changed requirement?
- **Route checked.** Bridge failing-tests row → [Human review pass](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/D-verification-evaluation/patterns/D05-review-pass.md#in-a-session-review-ai-changes-before-accepting-them), [Feedback into a check](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/I-reliability-recovery/patterns/retry-with-feedback.md#in-a-session-turn-feedback-into-a-check), with D02/AP10 retained for protected checks and permissions. The lookup, D family, and regression-testing use case reach the same review section.
- **Supported next step and limits.** Inspect each changed assertion and removed check; reproduce the original case and a neighboring behavior. A second bug needs its own expectation/reproducer; passing the selected checks is not proof of all correctness.

### Long session loses decisions or requirements (P-S02, P-04, P-28)

- **Constraints and distinction.** Person can inspect still-available conversation or project records and paste a handoff. Is the decision available, unresolved, or missing everywhere?
- **Route checked.** Bridge earlier-conversation row → [Session recovery](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/I-reliability-recovery/patterns/fresh-context-retry.md#in-a-session-recover-decisions-without-losing-useful-work); lookup, I family, E compaction, and debugging context use case reach the same session procedure.
- **Supported next step and limits.** Review the goal, governing decision and rationale, rejected option, useful work, open questions, and next check. Summarize aligned noise; reset only repeated reversion. Unavailable evidence still needs the user or another record.

### Agent reintroduces an explicitly rejected approach (P-S03)

- **Constraints and distinction.** Person controls the conversation, not the harness. Is this a settled rejected approach or unresolved product meaning?
- **Route checked.** Bridge repeated-approach row → [Session recovery](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/I-reliability-recovery/patterns/fresh-context-retry.md#in-a-session-recover-decisions-without-losing-useful-work); I family and lookup reach that section. For conflicting terminology, follow [Clarify domain terms](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/E-context-engineering/guides/clarify-domain-terms.md).
- **Supported next step and limits.** Preserve why the alternative was rejected, ask for a distinguishing prediction, then verify the actual changed result. Keep useful file edits separate from the conversation reset. This differs from enforcing a code-local marker.

### Generated diff exceeds review capacity (P-22, P-23, P-24)

- **Constraints and distinction.** Person can preserve the existing diff and review proposed restructuring. Is it planned work, a pending draft, or an already applied patch?
- **Route checked.** Bridge large-diff/cannot-keep-up rows → [Reviewable milestones](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/B-task-decomposition/patterns/B5-milestone-checkpoint.md#in-a-session-slice-a-change-for-review), [Human review pass](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/D-verification-evaluation/patterns/D05-review-pass.md#in-a-session-review-ai-changes-before-accepting-them); B and D family routes and the lookup point to those sections.
- **Supported next step and limits.** Save the existing work, identify the smallest observable outcome and real dependencies, propose a split/review order, and inspect both acceptance and retained behavior. Inseparable migrations need a declared integration checkpoint.

### Feedback is superficially satisfied (P-15)

- **Constraints and distinction.** Person can inspect the current parser and knows the retry-count contract. Which outcome, rather than implementation guess, is wrong?
- **Route checked.** Bridge feedback row → [Feedback into a check](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/I-reliability-recovery/patterns/retry-with-feedback.md#in-a-session-turn-feedback-into-a-check); I family, lookup, and D review cross-reference reach the same section.
- **Supported next step and limits.** Check explicit zero → 0, absent → 3, and positive two → 2. Merely changing the default to zero fails the absent case. A verbal prediction still requires execution or inspection of the actual revision.

### Domain meanings conflict (P-36)

- **Constraints and distinction.** A reporting owner can settle daily visits vs. raw scans. No agent harness changes are available.
- **Route checked.** Bridge domain-terms row → [Clarify domain terms](../../skills/sl-sensemaker/catalog/families/E-context-engineering/guides/clarify-domain-terms.md); E family and guide index and lookup reach the same guide.
- **Supported next step and limits.** Resolve a returning member's count with the owner, retain audit scans, record the recurring definition and consequential rationale, and verify a neighboring new-member case. If the owner cannot settle it, leave the affected requirement open.

### Team isolation with no applicable local guidance (P-46, P-47)

- **Constraints and distinction.** The concern is collaboration among people, not monitoring an agent.
- **Route checked.** Bridge team-isolation row still states no direct entry. K06 monitoring and D10 human calibration address other questions.
- **Supported next step and limits.** Do not substitute artifact persistence or personal review for a remedy to teammate isolation. This remains a guidance gap.
