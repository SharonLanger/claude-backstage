# Independent source review of SRC-0019

The review covers all twelve claims in the [source dossier](../sources/SRC-0019--repeatability-of-code-generation.md). I reopened the published ACM article, the UCL accepted manuscript, the authors' all-five agreement script and their supplemental PDF. The supplemental file's Git blob hash matched the independently fetched GitHub record, `8c0919d9f1bef4c5af83ea2a24718115da8023e2`. Checks use the authors' reported measurements and code definitions. I did not rerun the benchmarks. ## Numbered findings

1. **Provenance and rights — pass.** The ACM cover gives four authors, DOI 10.1145/3697010, the online manuscript date of 26 September 2024 and the publication date of 22 January 2025. The first article page grants CC BY 4.0. The UCL manuscript carries a narrower copying notice. The dossier assigns authorization to the published article and uses original exposition throughout. The versions and companion materials are one research group's evidence. [King's bibliographic record](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/an-empirical-study-of-the-non-determinism-of-chatgpt-in-code-gene/) · [ACM license notice](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/ws/files/365277933/An_Empirical_Study_of_the_Non-Determinism_of_ChatGPT_in_Code_Generation_Version_of_Record.pdf#page=3) · [UCL title page](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=1)
2. **SRC-0019:C01 — pass.** Sections 2 and 3 establish the identical zero-shot Python request, five baseline calls, one choice per call, model snapshot identifiers and top-p setting. Table 1 accounts for all 829 problems. Section 3.2 distinguishes the sampled 500 APPS tasks from the two complete benchmark test partitions. Section 2 documents the three-second limit and sequential execution of the test cases. [Methods and Table 1](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=3)
3. **SRC-0019:C02 — pass.** The implementation calculates the set of the five recorded outputs on each input, counts sets of size one and divides by the number of tests. For a nonempty suite, zero therefore means there was a disagreement on every test. A pair of candidates might still agree on every input. The distinction between unanimous results, pairwise results, exception handling and correctness is material to interpreting every OER figure. [§3.4.1](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=6) · [OER script, lines 39–101](https://github.com/ShuyinOuyang/LLM-is-a-box-of-chocolate/blob/main/syntactic_similarity_OER.py#L39-L101)
4. **SRC-0019:C03 and C11 — pass.** Tables 2–4 support the benchmark order, OER percentages, maximum pass-rate spread and both textual and structural scores. The HumanEval OER figure is 47.56% at the baseline temperature. The dossier correctly flags the broader introductory statement. The 39.63% HumanEval figure uses tasks with a full pass-rate spread as its numerator. [Tables 2–4](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=9)
5. **SRC-0019:C04 — initial clarification requested.** Section 3.3 lists 1.5 as the third temperature. The results and supplemental Tables 1–3 actually report 0, 0.5 and 1. The ledger correctly identifies the benchmark and rows. The accompanying narrative briefly said “that benchmark” after naming all three, obscuring which benchmark had mean pass rates 0.15, 0.16 and 0.16. Requested that the author name CodeContests there. Confirmed all three zero-temperature OER percentages and the two additional intermediate-temperature figures in the supplement. [§§3.3 and 4.2, Table 5](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=13) · [Supplement, Tables 1–3](https://github.com/ShuyinOuyang/LLM-is-a-box-of-chocolate/blob/main/Appendix.pdf)
6. **SRC-0019:C05 — pass.** Table 6 compares grouping choices in one API request against five separate requests. The CodeContests zero-temperature pairwise rates excluding exceptions are 0.81 and 0.46. Supplementary Tables 5–6 support the APPS and HumanEval pairs. The rounded zero maximum and positive worst-case fraction in the CodeContests single-request row warrant the dossier's caution. These data do not measure wall-clock parallelism. [§4.3, Table 6](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=14) · [Supplement, Tables 4–6](https://github.com/ShuyinOuyang/LLM-is-a-box-of-chocolate/blob/main/Appendix.pdf)
7. **SRC-0019:C06 and C07 — pass.** Checked model rows and OER columns in Table 7 and supplemental Tables 8–9. Table 7 supports the warning about reversed metric directions in the surrounding prose. Checked the three temperature-zero prompt rows for pass rate, LCS, OER and structural metrics in Table 8. The ledger limits these comparisons to the evaluated conditions. [Tables 7–8](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=18) · [Supplement, Tables 8–9](https://github.com/ShuyinOuyang/LLM-is-a-box-of-chocolate/blob/main/Appendix.pdf)
8. **SRC-0019:C08 and C09 — pass.** Section 7.2 retrospectively scores the best candidate against available tests and examines shared failure types. Sections 7.1 and 7.3 give developer and researcher advice and propose future voting and repair experiments. The distinction matters where a live team lacks the benchmark's scoring oracle. [§§7.1–7.3](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=22)
9. **SRC-0019:C10 — pass.** The description-length row of Figure 6 gives the cited signed correlations across CodeContests tasks. The telephone-pole and array examples appear in §4.4. Changing the wording or length of a particular task was outside this correlation analysis. [Figure 6 and worked examples](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10198256/1/3697010.pdf#page=15)
10. **SRC-0019:C12 — pass.** Listings 1–5 give seven acceptance cases and three illustrated candidates with scores of 3/7, 4/7 and 5/7. Listing 5 omits the integer check. Listing 4 includes the check but doubles the sum. The dossier appropriately limits the rank claim to the illustrated candidates. [Supplement, pp. 5–6](https://github.com/ShuyinOuyang/LLM-is-a-box-of-chocolate/blob/main/Appendix.pdf)
11. **Topic assignments — pass.** OB-06 receives direct evidence for same-prompt variability on historical GPT models and fixed Python tasks. BP-08 receives supporting evidence about diversity and retrospective candidate discrimination. The isolated repository patches, independent acceptance checks and manual comparison are clearly marked as catalog adaptations. The experiment leaves schedule, reviewer cost and performance on unseen requirements open.

## Disposition

Initial verdict **PASS WITH CHANGES**. The author named CodeContests in the temperature paragraph. I reread the revised sentence and checked its six values against the CodeContests rows of Table 5. **Final verdict PASS.** All twelve claims and both topic assignments are cleared for integration.
