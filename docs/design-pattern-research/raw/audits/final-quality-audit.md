There are 38 direct, 67 supporting and two contradictory source relationships. A direct relationship can document the existence of guidance or a result within a restricted setting. ## Limitations and verdict

All eight methods cite the same Anthropic product guide for at least one step. Separate practitioners, policy authors and researchers contribute other parts of the methods. The independent units of evidence are their observations and interventions. The eight citations to the guide contribute a single vendor perspective. An independent practitioner's account of restarting sessions and a controlled multi-turn study remain candidates for the next comparative reading. The strongest remaining gaps concern the causal effect of the methods on repository outcomes, unseen misalignment, human review fatigue and proposed mechanisms such as negative activation. Source access can also change. I reopened the debugging article via the publisher's documented cookie-fallback URL when the plain URL redirected. The individual audits cover the original claims beyond my sample. I did not re-run the underlying research experiments.
