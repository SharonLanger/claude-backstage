# Independent source review of SRC-0015

**Initial disposition — PASS WITH CHANGES.** The evidence for AP-04 comes from one exploratory workplace ethnography. Opening more kinds of tasks, keeping several processes active, and feeling stretched all appear in the investigator’s account. The interview gives no estimate of the proportion affected or of the consequences for delivery. Two wording changes were requested in findings 1 and 4. [Source dossier](../sources/SRC-0015--work-intensification-and-task-expansion.md) · [Haas News original](https://newsroom.haas.berkeley.edu/ai-promised-to-free-up-workers-time-uc-berkeley-haas-researchers-found-the-opposite/)
