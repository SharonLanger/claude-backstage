# Independent verification of PAT-0008

**PASS.** The contributor can use this entry to decide whether a proposed change is ready, explain an altered expectation, show what was checked and respond to later findings. Its strongest source is a real project's submission policy. The evidence leaves open how much reviewer time the routine saves and whether it reduces regressions. ## Findings

For an outside contributor with a previously unreported problem, the shared policy calls for an issue and approval before an AI-assisted PR. Compose requires the approved-issue label for every AI-assisted PR and prescribes local checks. The PAT instructs contributors to establish what their own project requires.
