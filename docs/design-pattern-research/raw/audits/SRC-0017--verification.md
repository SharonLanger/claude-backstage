## Correction record

**Final disposition — PASS.** The revised analytical reading records the four-token truncation test, its two recall values and the 1.80-point loss. I checked the revised paragraph against Appendix B.4 and the separate Table 2 row. The correction distinguishes mechanical truncation from semantic editing and keeps the two reported baselines separate. Finding 12 is closed. The publication’s conflicting headline and table values remain documented. This review verifies the published evidence and its interpretation.
