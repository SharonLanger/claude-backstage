# Independent source review of SRC-0022

**PASS after independent recheck.** Initial verdict was PASS WITH CHANGES. Two details in the dossier needed qualification, one about the developer's inferred intent in a worked example and one about the records released for replication. The study's numerical findings, units of analysis, and distinction between observable pushback and unobserved disagreement are supported. [Source dossier](../sources/SRC-0022--observed-developer-agent-misalignment.md) · [Conference record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1011/) · [Full proceedings paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1011.pdf)

## Provenance and scope

The conference record names Ningzhi Tang, Chaoran Chen, Gelei Xu, Yiyu Shi, Yu Huang, Collin McMillan, Tao Dong and Toby Jia-Jun Li in dossier order. The DOI, October 2026 proceedings, page range and author affiliations agree. The first page dates the conference October 24–29. The May 28 submission and August 31 revision are dates for the arXiv manuscript. The acknowledgments confirm the funding sources named in the dossier. I reopened the original PDF and compared the methods, results, limitations, case descriptions, pipeline sensitivity analysis and published coding rubrics to the dossier. I also checked the two earlier studies, author replication README and ACL rights policy. This is a source verification. It does not reanalyze raw sessions. [ACL record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1011/) · [PDF first page](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1011.pdf#page=1) · [Acknowledgments](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1011.pdf#page=10) · [arXiv history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29442)
